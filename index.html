About Us:

Our group began four years ago with just two people and a small step of faith. Although we didn't feel fully prepared, we trusted the Lord's leading and committed to faithfully teaching His Word. Through that journey, we learned that God's purposes are greater than our own and that He is faithful to work through willing servants.

Today, our group is built on the conviction of "Eternity Over Feelings." We believe genuine love is willing to speak the truth because it values a person's eternal relationship with Christ above temporary comfort. Our desire is to be sanctified by God's Word, to love Him above all else, and to love others by pointing them to the truth of Scripture. As 1 Corinthians 13 teaches, love rejoices with the truth and never fails. That is the foundation of who we are and why we do what we do.
