About Us

Who We Are

We are a young adults Bible study group committed to studying God's Word through in-depth, expository teaching. Our desire is to grow in our understanding of Scripture, encourage one another through fellowship, and pursue Christ together.

We believe in loving the truth of God's Word over our momentary feelings, seeking sanctification through Him and His Word, and putting God first and foremost in everything we do.

Eternity Over Feelings

Our name and mission reflect a simple conviction: eternity matters more than temporary feelings. We desire to live with an eternal perspective, grounded in Scripture and shaped by the work of the Lord.
