The Secret to an Overflowing Life
(Eph 3:13-21)

The English preacher George Duncan once went to visit some friends in the English countryside. For years they had lived in an old-fashioned, rundown building. To Mr. Duncan's surprise, he discovered that the house had been completely remodeled. The people had installed new lights, an electric stove, and many other pieces of modern equipment. But he was even more surprised to see the lady of the house still using a kerosene stove for cooking and oil lamps for lighting. After greeting him, she said, " George, don't look so confused. We've had a great change here, but we just haven't turned on the power yet."

This typifies the lives of so many Christians -- a great change has taken place in their hearts, but they haven't turned on the power yet! As a result, they never seem to be adequate for the demands that life places upon them. Knowing this tendency, (the tendency to faint because of difficulties, dangers and uncertainties v 13,) Paul prays that the saints may be able to "get our hands upon on (English words apprehend and comprehend comes from Latin, which means (to grasp, to lay hold of) this is the secret to the overflowing life.

Here in these passages in chapter 3 is the second prayer of Paul’s to the Ephesians. The first one was in chapter 1:15-19 which was a prayer of revelation (that you may know) but here in chapter 3 is a prayer of realization (That you may be). In chapter one it is a prayer that we might know what we are, in chapter 3 is that we might be what we know. One is empowerment, the other is enablement.

I. The Power of this overflowing Life is Found in Sound Theology -3:13-19

This prayer is a prayer Paul prayed for the Ephesian believers and therefore all generations of the church. We enter here what we call the “inner sanctum” of the Christian and spiritual life. This is to be entered into with great openness and readiness and humbleness. Question? Why are so many Christians frustrated, depressed and live in defeat? What is the ultimate cause? I think the one of the root causes is, our failure to realize what we are meant to be, and what God intended us to be. Paul is dominated in his letters and in his life with what he called, “the riches of Christ.”

It is possible to be in the Christian life and be resting on an initial experience of forgiveness that never grows beyond that. There is more to the Christian life than being forgiven and going to heaven. Paul prays that we grow in knowledge and understanding and enlightenment so that we may go on into deeper depths and discover greater heights, that we may know the hope of His calling.

Many Christians can be frustrated and depressed because of the ignorance to His word. But not only because of ignorance to the word but also, and we are all guilty of this: it is possible to have read the word and know them on the surface but have never grasped their meanings. There is a danger to be an expert in the letter and ignorant in the Spirit. If we only approach these texts with a mechanical mind our study leaves us with nothing. We are all guilty of knowing what it says, but totally ignorant in most cases of its spiritual content.

Paul’s whole meaning of these text acting as a pastor is that more than anything, that they might know the fullness of God. This is also my prayer and hope for you! Faith is something that appropriates truth and applies truth. This whole passage is to lead you into a deeper prayer life. The outcome of this is not load you down and see that this is not possible for you to experience, but that it is a fact and fully accessible to all of us who trust Him.

It is grievous and dishonoring to the Lord if we are anything less than this. This is where we ought to be and since we are not, then we are losing a great deal of the overflowing, joyful experience of the manifested presence of Christ in our lives.

A. The prayer offered up

1. a sense of intense constraint - 14 - bow my knees (Overwhelmed by the majesty of God's redemptive purposes and burdened at God's people's miniature appropriation of all the riches and resources available in Christ, Paul is driven to his knees.)

Spiritual posture in Ephesians is important. As dead sinners in chapter 2, we were lying in the grave of our sins, having been born-again, we are now sitting in the heavenlies in Christ (2:6), we are walking in the life, light and love of the Lord, so that we can stand against the devil (Eph. 6). But here in chapter 3, Paul is bowing in prayer.

Jews normally stood in prayer. Posture of the body isn't that important.

Someone has colorfully expressed this in a humorous little poem that reads as follows:

"The proper way for man to pray," said Deacon Lemuel Keyes; "The only proper attitude is down upon his knees."
"Nay, I should say the way to pray," said Reverend Doctor Wise, "Is standing straight with outstretched arms with rapt and upturned eyes."
"Oh, no, no, no," said Elder Snow, "such a posture is too proud. A man should pray with eyes fast-closed and head contritely bowed."
"It seems to me his hands should be plainly clasped in front, With both thumbs pointing to the ground," said Reverend Doctor Blunt.
"Last year I fell in Hodgkin's well headfirst, "said Cyril Brown. "With both my heels a-stickin' up, my head a-pointin' down; And I prayed right then and there; best prayer I ever said, The prayin'est prayer I ever prayed, a-standin' on my head."

2. a sense of intimate companionship - 14b,15

(a) a loving Father - 14b, 15a -- through Christ we are made partakers of the grace of adoption, by which they are taken into the number, and enjoy the liberties and privileges of the children of God, have His name put upon them, we have received the spirit of adoption, we now have access to the throne of grace with boldness, and are enabled to cry, Abba, Father, we are pitied, protected, provided for, and chastened by Him as by a Father: yet never cast off, but sealed to the day of redemption; and inherit the promises, as heirs of everlasting salvation.

ILL: A little blind girl was seated on her father's lap on the front porch when a long-time friend of the family quietly climbed the front steps, winked at his friend and then grabbed the blind daughter from her daddy's arms, ran down the steps and up the street.

He stopped suddenly, startled because the girl had made no effort to struggle or cry out. "Why aren't you frightened?" he asked. You didn't know who had you in his arms." I didn't have to know," she answered, "My daddy knew, and that was enough for me!"

(b) a lasting Family - 15b, a sense of belonging to the Forever Family. "None of us are worthy to be here, but praise God we belong!" We are all part of God’s Big Family Firm…Brothers and sisters with One Lord, One Spirit, One Baptism…Under the divine headship of Christ..

B. The power turned on -- 16-19

Four Progressive Steps to this fullness that Paul is describing.

1. Inner strength – v. 16 -- enduement of the Spirit's power (means to be reinforced, built-up)

(a) This settles the issue of personal adequacy. The sense of helplessness of not being able to cope, of running out of strength before the day is half done.

(1) care for the inner man - 2 Cor. 4:16 “ Do not be discouraged, even though the outward man is perishing, the inner man is being renewed day by day.

(2) controlled by the Holy Spirit - 5:18 “ And be not drunk with wine, which leads to debauchery, but be filled with the Spirit.”

(3) cultivate the Holy Spirit's presence - 4:30 “ Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in which you were sealed unto the day of redemption.”

(4) cooperate with the Holy Spirit's purpose - 1 Thess 5:19 “ Do not quench the Spirit.”

ILL: Story of man who bought Rolls-Royce. The company asked "How much horsepower does the engine have?" No reply. Several letters later he received a response. The letter said, "Dear Owner, we do not disclose the amount of horsepower our engines turn out, but rest assured it is adequate for every driving experience you encounter!

2. Indwelling Christ - 17 a – (Greek compound word) – living in a home / down. We would say, “to settle down and feel at home.” This means that Christ not only be in their conscience but that He be the Master of the house. That He becomes the Ruler and we become the slave. The heart of the Christian is to be a home, not a hotel!

There must be an experiential knowledge by faith within you. This is a special work of the Holy Spirit. What the Spirit does is make the Lord Jesus real to you. He gives a personal intimate knowledge that makes you aware that He is always there every minute of the day.

(a) This settles the issue of relational alienation. The sense of loneliness, isolation, separation is settled. Christ the Person of all Majesty is now living in me -- me, who before salvation was the person of all Misery -- so that now I have the sure Promise of all Mercy - Cols 1:27 – “Christ in you the hope of glory.” Christ in me is the guarantee that I will see and share in that glory which is realized in the Presence and Person of the God of Glory.

Poem: When wilt Thou come unto me, Lord? I languish for the sight; Ten thousand suns when Thou art hid, are shades instead of light. Nothing save Jesus would I know; My Friend, my Companion art Thou! Lord, seize my heart, assert Thy right and put all other loves to flight!

3. Incomprehensible love -- 18,19a

This is not to know about, but to know it! This isn’t to be analyzed, but to be realized!! The main problem with all of us is that we don’t know the Love of Christ. We have only glanced at it, but Paul is praying that they might gaze upon it! Know it to its fullest even though it surpasses knowledge.

When we come to a better understanding of this love we will be like Isaac Watts who said, “My richest gain I count but loss and pour contempt upon all my pride. Love is so amazing, so divine, demands my soul, my life, my all.” Has this love demanded your life? Do we really know what it means to be lost in this love and wonder and praise? Have we truly been broken by this love?

Love is personal, it’s always between two people. The love of Christ is towards you! I’m not asking do you know He died for you. I’m asking have you experienced this incomprehensible, unfathomable love for you? The Son of God, who loved Me and gave Himself for Me! The everlasting divine lover had manifested His love to Paul and it captivated his life.

(a) This settles the issue of unconditional affection. I need someone who knows all about me, and still will not reject me; someone who will love me in spite of my unloveliness and my unloveableness. There is only One who can do that – the Lord Jesus.

A love which is BOUNDLESS - breadth

His love is broad enough to encompass all mankind without distinction of color, class or conditions. It comprehends all nations, compasses all needs, confers measureless blessings for this life and the world to come. Oh that you may know the breadth!

A love which is ENDLESS - length

His love is everlasting. Jerm. 31:3. It is so long that it reaches from eternity past to eternity future. It is eternal love in the fountain of election, ceaseless love in the flow, endless love in its endurance, boundless love in that it exceeds all our sin and our guilt. Oh that you may know the length!

His love is FATHOMLESS - depth

Down from heaven’s glory to Bethlemhams stable, to Gethsemane’s agony, to Golgotha’s horror. It is deep enough to reach the most degraded, despicable, detestable sinner. Oh that you may know the depth!

His love is MEASURELESS - height

It is high enough to exalt us from the guilty sinners on death row to glorified sons enjoying eternal life in the never ending bliss of Heaven! 1 Samuel 2:8 -- "He raiseth the poor out of the dust, and lifteth up the beggar from the dunghill, to set them among princes, and to make them inherit the throne of glory. Oh that you may know the height!

"Even unto them will I give in mine house and within my walls a place and a name better than of sons and of daughters: I will give them an everlasting name, that shall not be cut off" (Isaiah 56:5).

The chief spiritual responsibility of the Christian should be to live in consideration and admiration of the wonderful love of Christ, to dwell on it in his thoughts until his heart is warmed, until his soul overflows with praise, until his whole life is constrained or influenced. He should meditate daily on its characteristics: its freeness, its pureness, its immutability. Christ loves us more than we love ourselves. He loved us even while we hated Him, and nothing can change His love for us.

Many years ago, Dr. Donald Gray Barnhouse pointed out that love is central to all the fruits of the Spirit listed in Galatians 5:22. He said, “Love is the key. Joy is love singing. Peace is love resting. Long-suffering is love enduring. Kindness is love’s touch. Goodness is love’s character. Faithfulness is love’s habit. Gentleness is love’s self-forgetfulness. Self-control is love holding the reins.”[iii]

In other words, there can be no Fruit of the Spirit, and thus no evidence of the presence of the Lord, apart from love. When the Fruit of the Spirit is absent, so is the Spirit Himself.

- The love of CHRIST is unknowable (Ephesians 3:19).
- The riches of CHRIST are unsearchable (Ephesians 3:8).
- The joy He gives is unspeakable (I Peter 1:8).
- The ways of CHRIST are untrackable (Romans 11:33).
- The grace of the Lord JESUS is unexplainable (II Corinthians 9:8).

The peace of CHRIST is unfathomable (Philippians 4:7).

- JESUS CHRIST Himself is unsurpassable (Exodus 15:11).

The hymn writer put it like this: “Could we with ink the oceans fill and were the skies of parchment made; were every stalk on earth a quill and every man a scribe by trade, to write the love of God above would drain the oceans dry; nor could the scroll contain the whole, though stretched from sky to sky!”

4. Infinite fullness - 19b

Amplified - "that you may be filled through all your being unto the fullness of God -- that is that you may have the richest measure of the divine Presence, and become a body wholly filled and flooded with God Himself."

The word “filled” means “to be full; or to be filled to the fullest.” It speaks of “total domination.” The person filled with anger is dominated by hate. The person filled with wicked desires is dominated by lust. The person filled with happiness is dominated by joy. So, to be “filled with the fullness of God” is to be dominated by Him.

Instead of thinking of our lives as mere bottles that can contain only a small portion of His fullness, think of our lives as ships, which have been sunk in the ocean of His grace, until we are filled completely with all that He is and can give us. We are ships that are dominated by the sea of His glory, driven about by the waves of His will, carried by the whim of His desires. We are His and there is no room in us for us or for anything else, but Him.

Total domination is what the Lord is after. He will not rest until He controls our lives to the fullest. The longer we strive to control our own loves, the harder He will fight us. It is only when we yield to Him fully, that can be filled with all He is.

This is a possibility for all of us. We have no right to be content with anything less than this.

II. The Praise that Sounds from Spiritual Doxology -- 3:20-21

A. The Direction of our Worship v. 20 - now unto Him

B. The Defense of our Confidence v. 20 - who is able to do

ILL: Alexander the Great, asked a man to give him his daughter's hand in marriage. The man agreed to do so but for a huge sum of money. The ruler consented and told him to request of his Treasurer whatever he wanted. So he went and asked for an enormous amount. The keeper of the funds was startled and said he couldn't give him that much without a direct order. Going to Alexander, the treasurer argued that even a small fraction of the money requested would more than serve the purpose. "No," replied Alexander, "let him have it all. I like that fellow. He does me honor. He treats me like a king and proves by what he asks that he believes me to be both rich and generous." In the same way, we should go to the throne of God's grace and present petitions that express honorable views of the love, riches, and bounty of our King!"

D. The Duration of our Praise v. 21– forever

Conclusion:

What must we do to see this overflowing spiritual life begin to deepen our walks and be rooted in the fullness of God?

1 Accept this scripture and believe this can happen to us. We must desire it, we must be dissatisfied where you are at! Casual Christianity isn’t Christianity at all, it’s the devils religion. We are meant to be filled with all the fullness of God. We are meant to know and begin to comprehend the measure of His love for us. When you do, then you begin to desire it, you long for it, then you know it's particularly and completely for you.

2 Realize you need to be strengthened by the power of the Spirit in your inner man. This is Paul’s first petition. V. 16 “That He would grant you according to the riches of His glory, to be strengthened with might by His Spirit in the inner man.” Because of our weakness and because of His greatness we need to be constantly strengthened so that we may know Him all the more. No man after being starved for several weeks is able to sit down and eat a heavy meal. It will kill him. He has to be slowly strengthened by small portions so that he can dine once again. None of us can experience this great manifestation and presence of God simply just how we are. We have to be prepared for this meal. So that, when He does come to us in this way we are ready for whatever He calls us to. This is a gift to us from God, given to us in His own time and in His own way. If you want this, just ask for it and He will give it.

3 Pray for it. Once you begin to realize the riches of this, once you see the beauty of this, you will ask for it, you will seek for it, you will desire it, you will become hungry, you will thirst, you will pray, you will agonize, you will sweat, you will grab ahold of Him like Jacob did and pray, “I will not let go til you bless me.”

He is able to do exceedingly, abundantly, above all, we can ask or think!

Thou art coming to a king, large petitions with thee bring, for His grace and power are such, none can ever ask to much!!! HE IS ABLE TO DO MORE THAN YOU CAN ASK IR THINK.

4 Keep on pushing in on Him. Don’t give yourself any rest, and don’t give God any rest… Don’t stop til you know it. Don’t stop til you come into the holy presence of Christ and be ravished by His love for you. Until you get lost in a sense of love and wonder and praise. Until you come to the awareness of a love that is unspeakable and full of glory.

When He answers this prayer, you are unmistakably aware of His presence, and He begins to uncover the dimensions of His great love for you, and at that moment you begin to embark upon this experience Paul is praying for these believers to come to reality of. Even so, should we pray this same prayer Paul prayed, for ourselves so that we may enjoy Him to the fullest in this world and then spend our eternity enjoying Him forever and ever, amen!
