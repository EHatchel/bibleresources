Called to Go, Chose to Run - Jonah 1:1-6

Main Point: Don't run from a God call

Introduction

Has anyone ever gotten a call that you didn't want to answer? You see that one name and you silence your phone. Not because you were busy or you didn't hear it, but because you knew what they were gonna say.

These gyms around here get you to sign up online, but when you go to cancel it you have to do it in person. Well there is a gym who didn't care I wasn't going until my card changed. They want their money and they call me 3 times a week. I see the call and I know what they are gonna say so I snooze that immediately. I kept telling myself I would deal with it later, but instead I went the opposite way.

That's Jonah, God doesn't give him a complicated task. He didn't speak in riddles or whisper what needed to be done. God just quite simply said "Go". Jonah quite simply responded with no, but not by words but rather action. By running, Not emotionally or by accident, but rather he plans a trip as far away from his call as possible.

The sad but honest truth is this, Jonah doesn't stop believing in God, he just stops obeying.

Point 1: The Call that Confronts (vv. 1-3)

Jonah was called by God to go preach to Ninivah, and just for a reference for some of your personal imagination, and Ninivah was like a modern day Memphis. This is how I always heard it.

Ninivah was the capital of the Assyrian kingdom. Which was 500 miles east of his hometown in Gathhepher. Meanwhile, where Jonah decided to escape God's calling was to go as far west as he could, which was 2500 miles to Tarshish. This would be in the southern part of Spain.

He did not like the Assyrians. Assyria was an idolatrous, proud, and ruthless nation bent on world conquest and had long been a threat to Israel. We learned this about the Assyrians in our clarifying the Bible study.

He went to get on a boat to escape God's presence. He wanted so badly to not go to what God was calling him to. He would travel an extra 2000 miles to run away. That's more work than just going and doing the work. The reason why might shock you which we will talk through later in Jonah.

The call placed on him was to go. How difficult is that call? Just go. Go and speak out. How many times in our lives have we had the opportunity where we went to speak? All the time and how many of us have turned that down to remain quiet. Plenty of time. I say it all the time but to share again, I'm an introvert. I don't wanna talk unless spoken to or if I really know somebody. If I place myself there would I be able to go and speak to an entire wicked city?

He was charged with sharing the word just like we are. If you think this text is just a story written about some guy then you are wrong. Look at what it's saying, it's written to all. Mathew 28:20 - teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. Are you doing that? That red text spoken from the lips of the living God on earth. Are you following those commandments or are you running? Not a trot or Jog but a full on sprint the opposite way to escape your responsibility.

The God call is not light nor is it an option. It's an obligation. Proverbs 15:10 "There is severe discipline for him who forsakes the way; whoever hates reproof will die."

Learning about this verse has been a heavy weight upon me recently. I talk about how available I always am, but it's because it's what I love to do. What happens when I receive a call that I won't love to do? Will I still be available or will I run from the call because of disagreement, discomfort, or change? I pray God gives me the strength to do what I do not want to do to advance the kingdom of God for his purpose.

Point 2: The Call that is Countered

Jonah tries to outrun his calling, He tries sleeping through conviction and escaping responsibility.

Something I found funny is that Jonah's name means dove, if you couldn't tell he's not very dove like. He has a Bad attitude - Hates another group of people - the Assyrians. He's disobedient, hard headed and definitely not peaceful. So God knowing his heart has to be extra to confront someone who is extra. 2000 miles extra to be exact.

There's times when God stirs up the sea. When we aren't paying attention or on the run. Everyone on the ship was in danger. Really meditate on this. Put yourself there.

The winds are hurling, the boat is in extreme conditions, rocking and turning every which way. Things are breaking, thunder is roaring, parts of the boat are snapping, the sail is slapping and whipping. People are running and yelling. The waves are capping over the edges of the boat and it looks as if they are about to sink and be stranded in a vast ocean.

Everyone is in a panic, why? There was a man running away from God. Do you think running away from your call could affect other people around you?

If you sit and think. What are some of the ways that you can "run" from your call? Opposition, physically, mentally, spiritually, lazily. Did you fit into any of these categories, I mean any of these. You're drained from work and family, responsibilities, chores, school, and you're mentally exhausted- you could miss your call.

Your rest day of doing nothing, productive in nothing, convincing yourself there is no harm in doing nothing - laziness - you could miss your call.

RUNNING!

Are you running now? And if so, who else is it affecting? Who is on your ship that's crashing and breaking and stirring up a panic? Are you married? Now your husband or wife is on your ship with you. Are you bringing them through your storm?

All the pagan sailors crying out to your God. Ironic while Jonah went and laid down and went to sleep.

Your God - the God of your imagination or the 1 true God? I don't know if it's wise to speak this way, but the truth of the matter is that some people think they know the one true God, but in actuality they only know the God they have crafted in their mind. The sailors were praying in this way. They were praying to their imaginary God they made. Once they realized nothing was changing, they went to awake Jonah and asked him to talk to his God. Show us his power. The power of the 1 true God. The God that speaks life into existence. The God who can calm a storm in an instance. The God who can part an entire sea right down the middle and then can collapse it on their enemies. This is the God that was supposed to be called on in this chaos and need of the people, but the prophet went to sleep.

The sleeping believer in the midst of work needed for the kingdom. There is work to be done, there are men and women awake and won't sleep until the work is complete. They are running around and reaching out. Meeting with people, teaching God's word, cleaning his church, cooking for His people, watching over the children, studying the bylaws and finances, counseling and correcting and you are asleep. You are content where you are and have drifted to sleep.

In the midst of chaos, where the world is needing it the most you have gone down and went to sleep. What is the excuse? The only thing you're doing is dreaming and you need to wake up. Get to work. The pagan sailors are literally praying to their false God asking for the one true God.

THERE IS A PRAYER MEETING UPSTAIRS AND HE IS ASLEEP.

Where have you grown spiritually asleep while chaos is all around you?

Point 3: The Call that Corrects (v. 6)

Captain of salvation - what do you mean sleeper? ARISE! Stop sleeping, there are things that need to be done. Arise and Call on your God. Listen to God say "Arise and call on your God".

David Guzik says this message,"I wish I could come up here and say that no one here is asleep. Assumed you are all awake cause you took time on a Saturday. But I can't assume that because that's not how this works."

In the same way I can't assume everyone here is awake just because you took time out of your busy schedule to come here on a Thursday or Wednesday. I can't assume that because that's not how that works. Some people are Dragged here, some people are awake but have drifted to sleep, some people are here to keep their Christian badge or their Christian streak going. To give off the Christian life but to live in complete opposition in sleeping. So this must be spoken about.

We are ignorant, insensible when we are asleep. It's time for us to wake up.

Charles spurgeon- a city suffers under a plague. The Christian asleep around the awake is a doctor in a plague asleep while people are infected all around the city and he has the cure in his pocket.

A passenger ship is about to throw itself into a rock bed where they would crash and surely look death in the face and the captain is asleep.

Prisoner in a cell ready to be executed. Terrified from hanging, but there is a man with a pardon from the governor and the letter is in the pocket of the sleeping warden of the prison.

So wake up and respond to your captain. You better listen to your captain. Call on your God. Come to him in faith. Ask.

Jonah learns that salvation is from the Lord. What wake-up call might God be using to get your attention?

We are not called to run away from God but rather we are to run to him.

Remember that call from the beginning? Now think of a call that you're anticipating, I remember I was part time at nike, and I did all my interviews for a full time spot. I did well in my interview, my boss gave me praise and told me to look out for a call. I sat by my phone at all times. Ringer on at all times anticipating this call. After a little while I answered so many scam calls I was so annoyed. Finally I got a call from a random number and the address was Beaverton, Oregon. I answered so fast and excited. I got the job. We should be anticipating God's call. We need to be awake, ready, and anticipating the call.

Application

Wake Up! Get to work!
