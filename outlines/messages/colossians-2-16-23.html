Colossians 2:16-23
Old VS New Covenant

V.16

Therefore = what is it there for.

Prior context is important to understand what Paul is about to say.

We covered last week the circumcision made without hands, which is Christ separating us from our sin. Having victory over the old law.

The old law or otherwise known as moses law consisted of 613 commandments, which guided the jewish people's everyday life in this time period. God committed to Moses providing these laws in prophecy to shine a light on what was to come. No matter the person, none could come close to obeying all 613 commandments. This was to steer people to believe they couldn't do it by themselves. The intent was to prepare a way for Christ to be revealed and be a perfected lamb to all people as we know with Paul's mystery revealed.

As we go through some of the laws listed here you realize the minute details of the laws. How something so miniscule could separate you from God. Christ has now taken the place of the law as we went through last week in Colossians 2:14: 'by canceling the record of debt that stood against us with its legal demands. This he set aside, nailing it to the cross. ' Taking its place because it needed a perfect and blameless person.

The legal demands spoke about show us 2 of the 3 laws

Civil - These laws were specific to the ancient Israelite society and dealt with issues like property rights, contracts, and criminal justice. They are not necessarily applicable to other societies or time periods, according to some theological interpretations.

Ceremonial - These laws governed the rituals, sacrifices, and festivals of the Israelites. They are often seen as fulfilled in Jesus Christ and no longer binding.

The 3rd law listed is the moral law that still stands today is the morality of creation.

Now knowing these laws, the ones that Paul references here are Ceremonial, these examples are… Read the verse again…

What do you think he means by “questions of food and drink?” and “a festival or a new moon or a Sabbath?”

He is discussing the religious rituals/acts that were to be done according to law.

The first one he mentions is the food or drink law from Leviticus 11

If you spend time reading this passage, you’ll see it lists a very strict diet. You could not eat camels, badger, hare, pig, eagles, vultures, and falcons to just name a few. It also says that you couldn’t eat anything from the water that didn’t have scales. The entirety of Leviticus 11 is the discussion of what could and could not be eaten. Why? Because according to law, eating all these animals would make you unclean/unrighteous in the sight of God. You can see how strict of a diet you had to have to be righteous in front of God.

The next one he talks about is festivals, which comes from Leviticus 23:2 ESV “Speak to the people of Israel and say to them, These are the appointed feasts of the Lord that you shall proclaim as holy convocations; they are my appointed feasts.

When you think of a festival, it is common to think of them being set on a singular day or time period for which to celebrate. This also applies here. The law stated there were appointed feasts held on specific days to worship. Time period were many would gather together in large and worship together on that day. No other day was permitted.

Paul talks next about “a new moon” or monthly offerings, which we can see in Numbers 28:11.

FUN FACT: The reason we see it called “a new moon” was because according to the Hebrew calendar, a new moon would be marked as the beginning of a new month. Also, the Hebrew word for month is hodesh, which literally translates to new moon.

This beginning of a new month was the designated time for the Israelites to bring an offering to God for sins and for worship. The Israelites at the time were NOT permitted to do it during other times as The New Moon marked as the designated day for God.

And alongside that he mentions the Sabbath, which as many of us know is considered the single day of worship or the single day of rest throughout the week.

This again constrained them to set times for the praise and worshiping of God.

So why does Paul talk about these things? Because all of these “requirements” are ceremonial, and not moral. Meaning that these rituals and laws have been fulfilled. The arrival of Christ has overcome those laws and they no longer are required for us to look righteous and clean in the eyes of God.

For example, the New Testament does NOT say anything about what food to eat or not eat for spiritual reasons. Jesus actually says the opposite.

Mark 7:15- “There is nothing outside a person that by going into him can defile him, but the things that come out of a person are what defile him.”

Romans 14:17 Paul even says - “For the kingdom of God is not a matter of eating and drinking but of righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit.”

Notice how he brings up food and drink here as these 2 are essential for us to survive. How many of you can go 1 day without thinking about eating and drinking? Not one, Paul knows the thought process of the believer and their worries.

Matthew 6:25-27 - “Therefore I tell you, do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink, nor about your body, what you will put on. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing? Look at the birds of the air: they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not of more value than they? And which of you by being anxious can add a single hour to his span of life?”

Matthew 6:33 - “But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.”

This is the answer to the new covenant in Christ. That you do not need to worry, for everything will be taken care of for you. Meaning all the laws are now fulfilled. So seek first the kingdom.

Paul also shuts down the idea of specific days for worship, like the festivals, the new moon, and the Sabbath. He is clear, in telling them that they no longer have to wait till the beginning of the month, or the first day of a week, or during a certain feast, to praise and confess their sins.

Romans 14:5 - “One person esteems one day as better than another, while another esteems all days alike. Each one should be fully convinced in his own mind.”

We apply this today, in that we don’t worship Christ only on Sundays. But everyday. Or we don’t fellowship and feast together once a month. But we come together and fellowship as a united body.

So going back to the beginning of the verse, “let no one pass judgment on you”.

Judge or (kree'-no) means finding fault with this or that in others, to pick out, select

The false teachers who were attacking the Colasse church were stuck in the legalism and ritualism of the old covenant, which would make them want to judge and find faults in the church members' ways. When in reality they are the ones who have been misguided and persuaded by their own self wisdom and "transcendent knowledge”.

How is Paul so confident in this? Read 17-19

V.17 - 19

“These are a shadow of the things to come, but the substance belongs to Christ.”

What causes a shadow? Something that exists.

A shadow can’t be formed from nothing. A person's shadow is present ONLY because the person exists. A person’s shadow can’t be their without the person.

Pay attention to the word substance here. The word substance here translates to (so'-mah) meaning the thing itself which casts the shadow.

Christ is the creator of the shadow, meaning the law (which is the shadow) no longer is binding to us since Christ (which Paul calls the substance) has arrived and fulfilled the law.

Hebrews 10:1 ESV - “For since the law has but a shadow of the good things to come instead of the true form of these realities, it can never, by the same sacrifices that are continually offered every year, make perfect those who draw near.”

Something I think we neglect is meditating on scripture, when I place myself in this text something cool comes to mind. Something has to be present to cast a shadow. But if something is present in the dark there can be no shadow, but Christ is the light of the world and a lamp onto your feet. Casting a shadow. He is the substance of light that casts these.

V.18

“Let no one disqualify you, insisting on asceticism and worship of angels, going on in detail about visions, puffed up without reason by his sensuous mind,”

Disqualify or (kat-ab-rab-yoo'-o) means to defraud, deceive the prize of victory, or “to deprive of salvation” (metaphor)

Paul is saying that the false teachers are deceiving the church of their victory with Christ.

We see he directly talks about some of the heretics’ beliefs that are attacking the Colossian church.

Asceticism

Angel Worship

Asceticism is the abstinence of worldly pleasures, or in simpler terms, the less you have and live for, the more spiritual you would become. Many who followed asceticism would give up any and all things earthly to prioritize the spiritual realm. They would even do painful or discomforting acts to themselves in their pursuit of spiritual goals and achievements.

And angel worship is the worshipping of any god that isn’t God the Father. If you are not worshiping God the father, it was what they considered a “sub-god”. And only some few who had the transcendent knowledge and wisdom and visions of these gods could guide you down the correct steps and rituals to get you closer to God the Father.

Do either of these sound like they line up with what Jesus taught? No.

Jesus tells us to deny ourselves, pick up our cross, and follow Him in Luke 9:23, but asceticism takes this to the extreme level. For example fasting until starvation, flagellation (the whipping of self), or self mutilation. No where in the Bible do we hear we need to do harmful acts to ourselves in order to be closer to the spiritual realm.

And Jesus clearly says that no one is to worship any god but God the Father in Matthew 4:10. - “Then Jesus said to him, “Be gone, Satan! For it is written, “‘You shall worship the Lord your God and him only shall you serve.”

William Barclay speaks on the end of this verse “going on in detail about visions, puffed up without reason by his sensuous mind,”:

The Gnostic prided himself upon special visions of secret things which were not open to the eyes of ordinary men and women. No one will deny the visions of the mystics, but there is always danger when a man begins to think that he has attained a height of holiness which enables him to see what common men-as he calls them-cannot see; and the danger is that men will so often see, not what God sends them, but what they want to see. There is the worship of angels [2:20]. Jews had a highly-developed doctrine of angels and Gnostics believed in all kinds of intermediaries. They worshipped these, while the Christian knows that worship must be kept for God and for Jesus Christ." (Colossians 2) [] Paul clearly states that the basic problem of the false teachers in Colossae was their egoistic, fleshly minds. Their professed "humility" was a cloak for excessive pride!

All of these ideas tried to back the legalistic idea that the acts, sacrifices, or rituals that WE do would put us in right standing with God. The more WE do for God, the more God will deliver us.

We know that's not true. We know that you can not earn your salvation through these works, it is a gift from God.

Ephesians 2:8-9 says clearly - “For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast.”

V.19

This verse directly links back to what he said at the beginning of the chapter in verse 2, when he stated his struggle with the church: “that their hearts may be encouraged, being knit together in love, to reach all the riches of full assurance of understanding and the knowledge of God’s mystery, which is Christ,”

Holding fast or (krat-eh'-o) means to take hold of, seize.

Paul, although never meeting the church members, has discerned that some of these members are not taking hold of the head, which is Christ, and his new covenant. These false teachers are persuading part of the church by leading them down growth through self wisdom, angel worship, asceticism, instead of what was the core of the church when it was founded.

If, for example, half of a church held fast to its core teachings of Christ as the head and the other half did not hold fast to the head and began listening and following these “new ideas” from man, could the church continue to grow? NO! The church is missing one key element that he highlights in this verse.

Growth in unity in the body of Christ.

Remember back to Ephesians 4 where Paul constantly talked about the importance of having unity in the church. “-from whom the whole body, joined and held together by every joint with which it is equipped, when each part is working properly, makes the body grow so that it builds itself up in love.”

Each joint and ligament in our body must have cohesion and work together to grow stronger. And in that same way, the church body must hold fast to the new covenant of God that comes from Christ, and to not be disqualified of their victory with Christ. Christ is the head of the church, meaning we are to follow the teachings of Christ. This is what knits the church body together.

1 Timothy 3:15 - “-if I delay, you may know how one ought to behave in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, a pillar and buttress of the truth.”

You have died with Christ, and he has set you free from the spiritual powers of this world. So why do you keep on following the rules of the world, such as, 21“Don’t handle! Don’t taste! Don’t touch!”? 22Such rules are mere human teachings about things that deteriorate as we use them. 23These rules may seem wise because they require strong devotion, pious self-denial, and severe bodily discipline. But they provide no help in conquering a person’s evil desires.

V.20

Why then do you—you who have attained your spiritual manhood—submit still to the rudimentary discipline of children? Why do you—you who are citizens of heaven—bow your necks afresh to the tyranny of material ordinances, as though you were still living in the world? It is the same old story again; the same round of hard, meaningless, vexatious prohibitions,”

As you see in these verses these are past pretense. You Died with Christ. I Have Been Crucified. When did we die? When we believed and trusted in him. This brings us back to the point last week. When we are unified in Christ, we are then united in his death, burial, and resurrection. And also remember that the word death speaks of separation, so believers now have been separated from this godless, fallen world system ruled by Satan.

V.21-23

The first criticism against philosophy or man-made approaches to God is that they enslave and subject men to the rules and teachings of men. Some in the Colossian church were returning to the rules and regulations of men as to how to please God. They returned to the idea...

• That man can please God by controlling the different weaknesses of the flesh—that man can secure God's approval by presenting a clean and pure body to God. Therefore, they began to come up with all the rules necessary to eat the right foods and to take care of their bodies and to live a clean life. Note how simply Paul puts it: they were saying "touch not; taste not; handle not.

Obviously these are good morally for a healthy body, but this still does not make a man righteous under God. Note a few things behind this response.

No matter how man treats his body, he is still imperfect and will still die on earth.

The approach to God by rules and discipline forces us to focus upon the rules instead of Christ. We have to keep our minds upon the rules or else we break them before we know it.

This does the opposite of what scripture teaches us about focusing our eyes on Christ. Just as we see in Hebrews 12:2, “looking to Jesus, the founder and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is seated at the right hand of the throne of God.”

The LMW puts the summary of this section as this,”The point is this: the philosophies and ideas of men enslave us: they force us to commit our lives to them and to focus upon them, and then they leave us enslaved to the bondage of death. Man-made ideas do not free us; they can carry us no higher than the man who made up the idea, and that height is not a height at all. It is a depth, the depth of death to which all men go.”

The question is this: If you are dead with Christ and never have to die again—if you are in union with Christ—if you are already acceptable to God...why then are you still trying to become acceptable to God? Your task is not to become acceptable to God. Your task is to focus upon God's Son, Christ, who has given you so much in His death and resurrection. Your task is

• To study His life and will
• To follow and pattern your life after His life
• To serve Him in His mission, giving all you are and have to reach all men with the gospel of eternal life

Life in Christ is not slavery; it is freedom.

Barclay writes Verse 23 this way,

”These things have a reputation for wisdom, with their self-imposed devotion and their flaunting humility and their stern treatment of the body, but they have no kind of value in remedying the indulgence of sinful human nature. “

Man's wisdom and will cannot destroy sin in the life of men. Man cannot keep from sinning through wisdom and will-no matter what he does. Man's wisdom and will cannot destroy death. A man cannot keep from dying.

Therefore, no matter how wise and how much man controls his body and life-no matter what kind of values and morality he follows; no matter what kind of self-made religion he worships-man still sins and still dies. He is still unacceptable to God.

Maint Point:

Paul is continuing forward with the superiority of Christ. He is so superior, He has victory over death and evil. Conquering and fulfilling the old law through perfection and sustaining the new law of love which knits the whole body together.

Application:

Christ came to fulfill the law, and he did exactly that. Now it's only about him.

Do not be caught up in the “religion” when it's about relationships.

Allow Jesus into your life and let him guide you.

Meditate on scripture.

Take rest in your acceptability from God.
