Ephesians 1:8–14
God, the Man, Is the Plan

Point 1: The Plan for the Believer

Verse 8 — Which He Lavished on Us

Over abundance / exceeded amount of grace.
It is not foolish to God to give this amount of grace.
Where sin abounds, so much more does grace.
In all wisdom and insight, He knew how much we would need.

Verse 9

We would have never known His will. It is a mystery to us because we do not have all knowledge and insight. Still, God wanted to make it known.

God showed His purpose through the life, death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus. And reveals His ultimate plan in Jesus.

We are in the median section of this passage. Notice at the beginning of this chapter he is speaking about the Father.

Verses 6–7, he speaks directly of the Son. Here in verses 8 and 9, we have a period of speaking about this great mystery that has been revealed to us before we get to the Holy Spirit.

Verses 8–9 are lavished grace and a mystery revealed. Paul goes into depth in verse 10.

2. The Mystery Revealed for the Believer

Verse 10

It will happen in the fullness of time.
Or, in other words, when Jesus returns.
At this monumental moment, all things will be gathered unto Him. Everything will be unified with Christ.

The verses in the passage almost point to some big problem that needs solved, and God says it will be revealed to us. We wonder what we have to do to solve this equation, and when it is revealed, God says, “I have revealed the answer,” and points to Jesus.

You want the answers? It all lies with Jesus.

The dispensation of the fullness of time, or other worded as the coming return of Jesus Christ.

Revealed in the mystery is Jesus, once rejected and turned away, will return as King. The Lord of all things on heaven and on earth.

Verse 11

What has been revealed in Christ is the answer, and He will unify all things unto Him on heaven and on earth.

Now because Christ is the answer, we have been given an inheritance.

You have been predestined, remember what we spoke about last time, according to the purpose of Jesus Christ — to unite all things in Him — who works all things according to the counsel of His will.

God is omniscient and knows all. Meaning He cannot learn. He already knew. He has chosen you.

A.K.A. predestined — but you also have chosen Him.

He has chosen us as creatures to be predestined unto Him, and you chose Him to be your Savior.

We work in congruent with God.

3. His Works for the Believer

Verse 12

Those who have trusted in Christ.

“To be the praise of His glory.”

The BBC puts it this way:

“To be the trophies of the grace of God.”

In verse 12, we see “we,” which commentary from David Guzik, William MacDonald, and other scholars determine to address specifically the early Jewish who responded to the gospel. Who, quite literally, were the first to hope in Christ.

This was to be to the worship and praise of His glory in a way that inspired others. Though not Jewish by today’s standards, I believe this still relates to us today. How we hope in Christ, through good works and fruit, is to the praise and glory of God.

Think of the last time you or someone else wore their salvation in a way that glorified the Lord so well it made others want to do the same.

Do you wear your salvation on your sleeve?

Verses 13–14

Look at the order in the verse to Gentiles:
They HEARD the gospel.
They BELIEVED in Christ.
They WERE SEALED WITH THE HOLY SPIRIT OF PROMISE.

The gospel has been revealed to you before sometime or another.

Colossians 1:23 (ESV)

“If indeed you continue in the faith, stable and steadfast, not shifting from the hope of the gospel that you heard, which has been proclaimed in all creation under heaven, and of which I, Paul, became a minister.”

You heard and believed. Put your faith and trust in Christ instead of yourself.

Romans 10:9–11 (ESV)

9 Because, if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. 10 For with the heart one believes and is justified, and with the mouth one confesses and is saved. 11 For the Scripture says, “Everyone who believes in Him will not be put to shame.”

This is THE gospel verse for salvation.

We should memorize where this Scripture is located and be filled with courage to share it with others.

You were sealed with the Holy Spirit.

The moment you hear THE truth, the gospel of your — and every and anyone's — salvation, and truly believe in Him, you’re sealed with the Holy Spirit.

The same Holy Spirit is John 14:26 that reads:

John 14:26

“But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in My name, He will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you.”

The Holy Spirit that is here to guide us, teach us, and be with us as believers. David Guzik called the Holy Spirit our “Down Payment” for a place in heaven. Nothing more needed.

“The word guarantee (‘down payment’) is used only in the New Testament of the Holy Spirit. He is our only down payment of coming glory; nothing else is provided — or needed.”

Evidence of the Spirit is guarantee of our inheritance of Heaven till we go there ourselves to be with the Lord.

And to that, praise be to the Lord.

Main Point

Paul expresses to the Ephesians the abounding grace the Lord has for them along with the inheritance He has for them based on the predestined plan to guarantee our inheritance with the Holy Spirit to glorify all the Lord has done.

Application

Be grateful for His grace.
Thank God for all He has done.
