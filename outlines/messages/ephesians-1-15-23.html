Ephesians 1:15–23
The Prayer of Paul

Verses 15–16 — Praise God

Because of your faith in Jesus and your love to one another.

30 years after the writing of this letter, Jesus rebukes the church because they left their first love.

Revelation 2:4

Jesus tells them good job. They were a hard-working church. They were about action and would not be pressed because they understood the truth. They did not compromise or waver whenever they were pressed on belief.

However, Jesus lets them know of their wrong. He tells them that they didn’t have the passion they once had prior. Their work was no longer motivated by love.

Is your work motivated by love?

If not, what are your motivations for doing so?

Paul is thankful for the Ephesians because of what he has heard about them living out the will of God.

Paul has a lot of lessons in his epistles, but a common one that I think is important. He prays for the Philippians and Ephesians. Why?

Not because they’re sick or need healing. Not because of a death of family. Not because of finances or because of lack of faith.

Because they are doing good.

It seems more often than not we pray for the bad and discard talking to God about the good. We forget to be thankful for us and the people in our lives that are following out the Scriptures.

Paul points this out in both of these prayers.

1. To Know God — v. 17

Paul knows that the Ephesians know God. They have experienced salvation and know God as a Savior.

They know His character and His love for them.

This is not enough, though, for Paul. He is mature and wise in faith. He wants them to know more of God. To have a greater revelation in the knowledge of Him.

Jeremiah 31:34 (ESV)

“And no longer shall each one teach his neighbor and each his brother, saying, ‘Know the LORD,’ for they shall all know me, from the least of them to the greatest, declares the LORD. For I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.”

It is one thing to know God. And another to know God personally.

To know about God or to know the characteristics of God. The love of God. The grace of God. The mercy and forgiveness of God. His pursuit of you. To fully trust in Him. To surrender your life.

This really is to know God.

If God is a personal God, then we must be personal.

God is not just in our minds. But the Savior of the world that died on a cross. The only true living God.

What does surrender look like to you?

We must always be increasing in the knowledge of God.

Paul shows us how through his prayer: Wisdom

The LMW commentary defines it this way:

Wisdom can best be understood by the single words what and how. Wisdom means knowing what something is, what is behind something, and what can be done. It is knowing how to use or relate to something.

Therefore, spiritual wisdom means:

● Knowing who God is and how to relate to Him.
● Knowing the truth and how to use it.
● Knowing what to do and how to do it.
● Knowing how to live more and more fruitful lives—for the glory of God and for the welfare of men.

If knowledge is listening to facts, wisdom is learning to use those facts.

The life you want to live is close. You have to be a wise man in the kingdom.

Using the facts about God that you have understood, and increasing your knowledge of God and His will and living it out shows true mature wisdom.

The Spirit of Revelation

It is written as spirit because it is the Holy Spirit that reveals God to you.

1 Corinthians 2:9–12 (ESV)

But, as it is written, “What no eye has seen, nor ear heard, nor the heart of man imagined, what God has prepared for those who love Him”— these things God has revealed to us through the Spirit.For the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God. For who knows a person’s thoughts except the spirit of that person, which is in him? So also no one comprehends the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God. Now we have received not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, that we might understand the things freely given us by God.

Just as it is written in the Scriptures, the Spirit of God:

Searches — The Holy Spirit searches your mind and heart to cleanse evil from them and plant the push of righteousness in you.

Gives understanding of God — Reveals God’s nature and character for us to understand and love Him intimately.

Seeks after God — Gives us a heart to want to have a relationship with our Father in Heaven.

2. To Know Purpose — v. 18

Eyes of Understanding Being Enlightened

Greek translations say heart.

Where does your emotions stem from?

Is your heart the center point of emotion or is it your mind?

Are they connected as one?

Do they stem off of each other?

The eyes used here are spiritual.

What you once saw, heard, and maybe even spoke about, now you really have an understanding about spiritually.

Like the old phrase and also played in the older cartoons: a lightbulb just lit up above my head. I had trouble with the comprehension of what I read or was told, but now I really get it.

How can someone be so smart, and yet be so blind to the truth?

2 Corinthians 4:4 (ESV)

“In their case the god of this world has blinded the minds of the unbelievers, to keep them from seeing the light of the gospel of the glory of Christ, who is the image of God.”

When Paul wrote that Satan has blinded the minds of unbelievers, the Greek points out that this doesn’t mean one who is unable to see, rather he has been intentionally blinded.

He hasn’t lost his sight, but rather he has no eyes to see.

Satan has disrupted the unbelievers’ thoughts, reasoning, opinions, feelings, and beliefs. For those of you that truly have an intimate relationship with the Lord, didn’t this happen?

You learned about God and thought you understood. Lived a life thinking you knew God but your life didn’t have any change or difference in the slightest.

Which didn’t make sense because you’ve read that you must be different and you’ve heard the experiences that other people had with God.

Then you made a decision to truly surrender and trust the Lord, and the eyes of your heart, or the eyes of your understanding, were opened and you got it.

This is not limited to salvation. It is many of God’s truths in our lives.

I pray that if you haven’t experienced this, that this would be your life. Cause it is truly life-changing.

This is what Paul is praying here for the Ephesians.

What is His inheritance? Because it is different than in verse 11.

3. To Know the Power of God — v. 19

The work of His great might that He worked in Christ when He raised Him from the dead. Do you feel powerful?

The same power that raised Jesus from the dead lives in you.

Seems pretty powerful to me.

Do you feel powerful now?

Now that that knowledge has been revealed to you.

Notice Paul in saying he hopes they understand the power God has and not asking for more power.

The power of God is all you need. You have sufficient power.

Paul prays that the church would understand your power, and I’m praying for that of you now today.

That we would gain a new wisdom of Your power and realize the capacity that Your words hold in our lives.

That what You will, we will work out Your calling in our lives because we know Your power and that You wouldn’t will us to do anything outside of our capacity.

How much more confident we should feel knowing that God doesn’t will us to do anything that He knows we can’t do.

So if God calls you to do something, He knows you are very much capable.

God’s Plan Is the Church — v. 22

“I don’t have to go to church.”

God specifies He put the church under Jesus.

That should show some great importance to us and the importance of the church to God.

1 Timothy 3:15 (ESV)

“If I delay, you may know how one ought to behave in the household of God, which is the church of the living God, a pillar and foundation of the truth.”

Main Point:

Paul prays of the good works the Ephesians are doing and asks God to reveal Himself personally to them. To give them a great purpose and to reveal the power that has been given to them through Christ’s resurrection.

Application:

Be faithful.

Seek and pray over the insight and wisdom of what the Lord is working in our lives. Remember the power.

Be personal.

Pray for good and bad.
